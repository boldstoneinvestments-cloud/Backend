## Company

Boldstone Investments operates in Uganda and works with coffee farmers, investors, and trade partners. Boldstone's farmer-first approach supports smallholder farmers with markets, coffee processing, farming knowledge, and sustainable coffee production.

Boldstone works to build coffee value chains and coffee processing infrastructure in Uganda. Boldstone partners with farmers, investors, trade partners, farming groups, SACCOs, and people interested in coffee farming.

## Coffee Estate And Farming Plans

The Boldstone coffee estate is in Kyenjojo District in Western Uganda. Kyenjojo is a coffee-growing region known for fertile soils, favorable rainfall, and Robusta coffee production.

Boldstone offers professionally managed coffee farming through land leasing and farming plans. Customers participate in a managed coffee farming program; they do not purchase the land.

Depending on the selected plan, the program may include:

- Access to agricultural land through a lease or farming arrangement.
- Coffee seedling establishment.
- High-quality coffee seedlings.
- Indigenous shade tree seedlings.
- Land preparation.
- Farm management.
- Agronomy supervision.
- Farm maintenance and monitoring.
- Ongoing farming support.

The Monthly Subscription Plan is designed for customers who want a manageable monthly commitment. During the first six months, payments contribute toward land preparation, seedling procurement, and farm establishment. From Month 7, Boldstone begins establishing the one-acre coffee farm while the subscription continues.

The Annual Plan is designed for customers who want their coffee farm established without waiting for a phased setup period. Plan inclusions and prices are the values currently displayed on the public lease page.

Customers do not purchase agricultural land through the farming program. Where land access is included, the applicable lease or written land-use agreement governs the arrangement.

## Farming Risks

Coffee is a perennial crop and requires time to establish before commercial production. Planting, growth, maturity, and harvest depend on weather, rainfall, soil conditions, altitude, disease pressure, agronomic practices, and other circumstances.

Boldstone uses reasonable professional efforts to manage farms using appropriate agronomic and environmental practices. Agricultural production has biological, climatic, and market risks. Boldstone does not guarantee a particular yield, harvest volume, coffee price, or financial return.
